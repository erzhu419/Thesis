[P0081] 从仿真到现实的公交系统驻站控制方法研究

[P0082] 摘要：随机客流和路况变化使公交车头时距失衡，增加串车风险和乘客等待时间。驻站可调节车辆离站时刻，但受到车辆与站点差异、环境条件及动作上限的制约。强化学习可依据状态选择动作，却难以在实际运营中反复试错，最终策略又面临训练与使用环境的分布偏移，使其现实部署面临困难。本文围绕从仿真到现实的驻站控制，先研究未预先获得目标域训练数据时的平稳随机鲁棒学习与非平稳随机适应学习，再研究获得目标域离线数据后的跨保真训练及残差世界模型修正。本文以公交到站且完成必要上下客后为决策时刻，以公交与站点信息、前后公交车头时距、乘客时间成本为强化学习在途控制模型的输入，公交驻站时间为控制模型输出。实验过程采用长沙市六条双向公交线网相关数据，搭建粗粒度快速公交仿真系统用于低成本训练，并采用微观交通仿真工具（Simulation of Urban MObility，SUMO）构建高精度公交系统仿真，作为现实环境的高保真代理。本文前两部分研究内容先采用快速公交仿真系统训练相应的鲁棒强化学习，再到SUMO进行系统测试；在后两部分研究内容中，跨保真迁移是采用SUMO离线数据以及与快速仿真系统在线交互数据进行强化学习策略训练，然后在未参与训练的SUMO运行场景中进行策略评价。本文主要研究内容与结果如下。

[P0083] （1）针对双向发车环境导致的特定时段内某些车辆的运营数据丰富程度低于平均水平，且客流与路况的高方差随运营时间累计的问题，设计了考虑车辆类别与站点类别的特征嵌入与连续状态的共享驻站策略，以加权的候车与车内时间作为乘客广义时间成本，并提出了单智能体的鲁棒集成软演员评论家方法（Robust Ensemble Soft Actor-Critic，RE-SAC）。实验结果显示：在常规运行环境、客流激增和路况快速变化情况下，RE-SAC相较于Soft Actor-Critic（SAC）方法分别降低5.51%、1.41%和4.91%的人均广义时间成本。同时，RE-SAC相对SAC的平均计划时距偏差和公交车头时距不均衡度分别降低5.00秒和7.36秒。

[P0084] （2）进一步考虑公交客流与路况 的随机统计规律随时间变化，通过环境的识别和置信模式调节形成条件自适应，提出了贝叶斯自适应策略正则化（Bayesian Adaptive Policy Regularization，BAPR）；同时，针对客流与路况均值偏差比较大的场景，提出了因果符号贝叶斯自适应策略正则化（Causal-Symbolic Bayesian Adaptive Policy Regularization，CS-BAPR）。实验结果显示：以车头时距均衡度 为目标，在正常乘客需求、持续高需求、短时极端需求、以及单次与重复乘客需求变化下，CS-BAPR相对于BAPR降低平均时距惩罚的幅度在58.82%与69.79%之间。与RE-SAC的匹配比较显示，控制奖励与具体运营指标仍存在取舍，其结构化输出并非在每种条件下都带来额外收益 。改用乘客广义时间成本后，CS-BAPR相较于BAPR人均广义时间成本增加0.068%至0.268%，表明该组时距控制收益未转化为乘客时间收益 。

[P0085] （3）针对现实目标环境难以持续通过试错采集训练数据的问题，在混合离线与在线强化学习（Hybrid Offline-and-Online Reinforcement Learning，H2O）及其改进框架（Improved Framework for Hybrid Offline-and-Online Reinforcement Learning with Dynamics Gaps，H2O+）基础上建立跨保真公交系统强化学习驻站控制训练流程。采用SUMO高保真离线数据与快速仿真系统低保真在线交互数据进行强化学习策略训练，由在线交互数据分布与离线数据分布的比值决定了每条在线交互数据在每次策略更新时采用的更新步长。实验结果显示：在十个新环境块、三档需求和九种控制器的1350次组合评价中，完成保障相对相同学习策略的直接参照，使窗口内人均广义乘客时间减少92.12秒、下降3.71%，完成率提高0.466个百分点。在使用同一批目标离线数据、策略与评论家更新预算匹配的比较中，混合训练方案相对所测纯离线方案有正收益。 

[P0086] （4）针对源域和目标域环境不一致导致的公交运行转移规律失配，提出基于模型修正的世界模型方法（Model-Corrected World Model，MC-WM），利用现实数据，用残差世界模型对仿真系统偏离现实的部分进行修正，包括状态，动作，下一时刻状态以及奖励。将训练仿真中的道路平均速度、乘客OD强度、路口通行影响和必要停站时间同时改变，定义为联合源参数偏差。实验结果显示：以乘客广义时间成本为目标，在正常标定、联合50%和联合75%三种条件下，相对采用共同完成保障的Daganzo式规则，人均广义乘客时间分别减少57.67、70.67和76.26秒。采用车头时距奖励训练时，联合75%偏差下完成保障执行的人均广义乘客时间相对H2O+平均减少13.36秒。上述结果支持目标离线数据辅助源仿真训练的服务作用，并表明该作用受到训练目标与执行规则的共同影响。共同源采样与执行条件下的完整控制器补充比较中，MC-WM相对H2O+和纯源SAC的人均广义乘客时间分别平均减少23.02秒和40.25秒，乘客完成率分别提高0.1581和0.2626个百分点。

[P0087] 关键词：公交驻站控制；鲁棒强化学习；分布偏移；仿真到现实；世界模型

[P0089] Research on Bus System Holding Control Methods from Simulation to Reality

[P0090] Abstract: Random passenger demand and changing road traffic disrupt bus headways, promote bunching, and increase passenger waiting time. Holding control regulates departure times, but its effectiveness depends on vehicle and stop characteristics, operating conditions, and the maximum permitted holding time. Reinforcement learning can select actions from operational observations, yet repeated trial-and-error learning on an operating fleet is impractical, and the resulting policy may encounter a distribution shift between training and deployment environments. This thesis studies sim-to-real bus holding control: robust learning under stationary stochastic conditions and adaptive learning under non-stationary stochastic conditions first address settings without pre-collected target-domain training data; cross-fidelity learning and model correction then incorporate target-domain offline data.

[P0091] An arrival-event-based control model takes decisions after required passenger boarding and alighting and connects vehicle and stop information, forward and backward headways, a holding action, and passenger time costs. Multi-line experiments use an existing Changsha road network, bus routes, origin-destination (OD) demand, and timetables. An in-house simplified simulator supports low-cost training. Simulation of Urban MObility (SUMO) represents road traffic and vehicle interactions in greater detail and serves as a simulated proxy for the real operating environment. Cross-fidelity transfer combines SUMO offline data with new data collected through online interaction in the simplified simulator and evaluates the resulting policy in SUMO operating scenarios not used for training. All bus-control studies in the thesis use a single holding action. The main contributions and results are as follows.

[P0092] (1) To address differences among vehicles and stops and fluctuations in action-value estimates during stochastic operations, this thesis proposes a shared holding policy combining categorical feature embeddings with continuous observations and develops Robust Ensemble Soft Actor-Critic (RE-SAC). Generalized passenger time is defined as twice waiting time plus onboard time. With the same learner and training settings, learned embeddings reduce generalized passenger time relative to one-hot encoding by 5.51%, 1.41%, and 4.91% under routine operations, demand surges, and rapid switching. In the multi-line holding comparison, RE-SAC reduces mean planned-headway deviation and forward-backward imbalance by 5.00 and 7.36 seconds relative to SAC under the headway objective, while operating objectives retain tradeoffs.

[P0093] (2) To address changing operating laws and the limited ability of holding to alter departure times, this thesis proposes Bayesian Adaptive Policy Regularization (BAPR), using environment identification and operating-mode confidence adjustment, and subsequently develops Causal-Symbolic Bayesian Adaptive Policy Regularization (CS-BAPR). Across routine demand, single and repeated environmental changes, sustained high demand, and brief extreme demand, training stabilization reduces the mean headway-reward penalty relative to the preceding BAPR configuration by 58.82% to 69.79% with a single holding action. Matched comparisons with RE-SAC show tradeoffs between control reward and operational indicators, while structured policy outputs do not provide an additional benefit in every condition. With passenger-time training, generalized passenger time increases by 0.068% to 0.268% relative to the preceding BAPR configuration, so the headway gains do not extend to passenger time.

[P0094] (3) To reduce dependence on repeated trial-and-error data collection in the target operating environment, this thesis develops a bus-specific cross-fidelity workflow based on Hybrid Offline-and-Online Reinforcement Learning (H2O) and the Improved Framework for Hybrid Offline-and-Online Reinforcement Learning with Dynamics Gaps (H2O+). Fixed SUMO offline data and simplified-simulator interaction jointly support policy training. Simultaneous-arrival encoding, a single action that adds either no extra holding or a positive holding duration, and a completion reserve for late-operation states with low onboard loads and insufficient following service form the complete controller. In 1,350 paired evaluations covering 10 new environment blocks, three demand levels, and nine controllers, the reserve reduces within-window generalized passenger time by 92.12 seconds, or 3.71%, and increases completion by 0.466 percentage points relative to the direct parent with the same learned policy. With the same target offline data and matched actor and critic update budgets, hybrid training improves over the tested offline-only scheme.

[P0095] (4) To address source-target transition mismatch, this thesis proposes the Model-Corrected World Model (MC-WM), selecting residual correction or direct target modeling using held-out prediction errors and weighting one-step model samples through log-confidence evaluation. The bus extension uses fixed target offline data and hybrid event models, with a normalized confidence backup limiting attenuation of model updates. With a single holding action and a passenger-time objective, generalized passenger time decreases relative to a Daganzo-style rule with the same completion reserve by 57.67, 70.67, and 76.26 seconds under calibrated, joint 50%, and joint 75% source-parameter mismatch, respectively. Joint mismatch changes road speeds, passenger OD demand, intersection delays, and required stopping-service times together. Under headway-reward training and joint 75% mismatch, MC-WM reduces generalized passenger time by 13.36 seconds relative to H2O+ with the completion reserve, but does not improve the headway reward. Benefits thus depend on both training and execution. A supplementary final-controller comparison with common source sampling and execution rules yields mean reductions of 23.02 and 40.25 seconds in generalized passenger time relative to H2O+ and source-only SAC, with completion increases of 0.1581 and 0.2626 percentage points, respectively.

[P0096] The thesis connects stationary robust control, non-stationary adaptation, cross-fidelity transfer, and world-model correction through target-domain data availability. Its central mechanism combines records from a detailed simulator with interaction in a simplified simulator to learn holding policies that generalize to unseen SUMO scenarios. These findings provide a basis for future bus-control studies combining real operation records with simulator interaction.

[P0097] Keywords: bus holding control; robust reinforcement learning; distribution shift; sim-to-real transfer; world models
