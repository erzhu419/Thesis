（2）针对客流与路况统计规律随时间变化、历史经验难以充分反映当前运行条件的问题，在贝叶斯自适应策略正则化（Bayesian Adaptive Policy Regularization，BAPR）的研究基础上，提出因果符号贝叶斯自适应策略正则化（Causal-Symbolic Bayesian Adaptive Policy Regularization，CS-BAPR）。根据近期奖励与价值估计分歧调整训练中的保守程度，将训练稳定化、可学习基准动作与神经网络修正相结合，并利用软符号约束引导驻站动作及其对状态变化的响应。公交评价采用训练完成后的固定策略，实验结果显示，以乘客广义时间成本为训练目标，在正常需求、单次与重复需求冲击、持续高需求和短时极端需求六类场景下，CS-BAPR相较于RE-SAC的人均广义时间成本平均降低0.083%至0.549%，平均计划时距偏差和前后车头时距不均衡度分别减少7.35至12.09秒和11.88至20.08秒。上述结果表明，CS-BAPR在所测客流变化条件下进一步改善了RE-SAC的平均乘客服务与间隔控制。

（3）针对目标环境已有运行记录、但难以持续试错获取新增训练数据的问题，在混合离线与在线强化学习（Hybrid Offline-and-Online Reinforcement Learning，H2O）及其扩展H2O+框架基础上，建立公交驻站的跨保真混合训练流程。利用固定SUMO离线数据初始化策略，再结合快速公交仿真中新采集的交互数据继续训练，并针对同时到站车辆的决策组织，以及是否额外驻站和驻站时长的动作表示完成公交适配。在目标离线数据相同、策略与价值网络更新次数相同的五次独立训练中，混合训练相较于所比较的纯离线训练方案，在正常和1.25倍需求下的人均广义时间成本分别平均减少16.66秒和19.75秒。进一步针对指定时段内有前车、无在途后随车辆、载客率不高于25%且仍有可服务客流的状态，设置行程完成保障规则。在保持同一学习策略不变的条件下，将最低附加驻站时长由20秒提高至60秒，并扩大启用时段；在十个新场景组、0.75倍、正常和1.25倍需求的评价中，人均广义时间成本平均减少92.12秒（3.71%），行程完成率提高0.466个百分点。上述结果分别说明了混合训练与行程完成保障对乘客服务的改善。

（4）针对跨保真训练中源仿真标定不足造成的运行预测偏差，提出基于模型修正的世界模型方法（Model-Corrected World Model，MC-WM）。利用固定目标域离线数据，分别学习源预测的残差修正与直接目标模型，依据未参与模型拟合的验证数据选择预测方案，并生成一步转移辅助价值学习。公交模型联合预测后继事件、车辆运行状态、事件间隔和乘客成本；通过归一化置信度递推更新，缓解模型样本权重持续减小的问题。实验同时改变源仿真的道路速度、乘客OD强度、路口延误和必要服务时间，设置联合源参数偏差。以乘客广义时间成本为训练目标，在正常标定、联合50%和联合75%偏差三种条件下，20次独立初始化的比较中，MC-WM相较于采用相同执行规则的无世界模型混合训练基线，人均广义时间成本分别平均减少51.14秒、69.93秒和87.05秒。另在源仿真交互量与执行规则一致的完整控制器比较中，MC-WM相较于H2O+和纯源SAC分别平均减少23.02秒和40.25秒成本，行程完成率分别提高0.158和0.263个百分点。结果表明，目标域离线数据支持的模型辅助训练能够在所测条件下缓解源仿真标定不足的影响，并改善乘客服务。

# English abstract: replacement items (2)–(4)

(2) To address changes in passenger-demand and road-traffic statistics that reduce the relevance of historical experience, this thesis develops Causal-Symbolic Bayesian Adaptive Policy Regularization (CS-BAPR) based on Bayesian Adaptive Policy Regularization (BAPR). Recent rewards and disagreement among action-value estimates are used to adjust conservatism during training. Training stabilization is combined with a learnable baseline action and a neural correction, while soft symbolic constraints guide holding actions and their responses to changes in the state. Bus policies are evaluated with their trained parameters fixed. With generalized passenger time cost as the training objective, CS-BAPR reduces mean per-passenger generalized time cost by 0.083% to 0.549% relative to RE-SAC across six scenarios covering routine demand, single and repeated demand surges, sustained high demand, and brief extreme demand. Mean planned-headway deviation and forward-backward headway imbalance decrease by 7.35 to 12.09 seconds and 11.88 to 20.08 seconds, respectively. These results show average improvements over RE-SAC in passenger service and headway control under the tested demand changes.

(3) To use existing target-environment records without relying on continued trial-and-error data collection in that environment, this thesis establishes a bus-specific cross-fidelity training procedure based on Hybrid Offline-and-Online Reinforcement Learning (H2O) and its extension, H2O+. Policies are initialized using fixed SUMO offline data and then trained further with newly collected interactions from a fast bus simulator. The bus adaptation organizes decisions for simultaneously arriving vehicles and represents both the choice of no additional holding and continuous holding durations. Across five independent training runs using the same target offline data and matched numbers of policy- and value-network updates, hybrid training reduces mean per-passenger generalized time cost by 16.66 seconds under routine demand and 19.75 seconds under 1.25-times demand relative to the tested offline-only scheme. A trip-completion safeguard is also introduced for designated operating periods when a preceding bus is present, no following bus is en route, the onboard load does not exceed 25% of capacity, and serviceable passenger demand remains. Keeping the learned policy unchanged, increasing the minimum additional holding time from 20 to 60 seconds and extending the activation period reduce mean per-passenger generalized time cost by 92.12 seconds (3.71%) and increase trip completion by 0.466 percentage points in evaluations across ten new scenario groups and demand multipliers of 0.75, 1.00, and 1.25. The two comparisons separately establish the service improvements associated with hybrid training and the execution safeguard.

(4) To address prediction errors caused by insufficient source-simulator calibration during cross-fidelity training, this thesis proposes the Model-Corrected World Model (MC-WM). Fixed target-domain offline data are used to learn both residual corrections to source predictions and a direct target model. Prediction errors on validation data not used for model fitting determine which model generates one-step transitions for value learning. The bus model jointly predicts subsequent events, vehicle operating states, event intervals, and passenger costs. Normalized recursive confidence updates mitigate the progressive attenuation of model-sample weights. Joint source-parameter mismatches are introduced by changing road speeds, passenger OD demand, intersection delays, and required service times together. With generalized passenger time cost as the training objective, comparisons across 20 independent initializations show mean cost reductions of 51.14, 69.93, and 87.05 seconds relative to a hybrid-training baseline without a world model under calibrated, joint 50%, and joint 75% source-mismatch conditions, respectively, using the same execution rules. A separate comparison of complete controllers with matched source-interaction amounts and execution rules yields mean reductions of 23.02 seconds relative to H2O+ and 40.25 seconds relative to source-only SAC, with trip-completion increases of 0.158 and 0.263 percentage points, respectively. The results support the use of fixed target-domain offline data for model-assisted training to mitigate the effects of insufficient source calibration and improve passenger service under the tested conditions.
